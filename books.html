Business Plan Essentials

Available

How to build a lender- and investor-ready business plan.

Business Plan Essentials walks readers through the full structure of a professional business plan—from executive summary to financial projections—using plain language and practical examples. It is especially helpful for entrepreneurs who are preparing plans for banks, immigration programs, or private investors and want to understand what decision-makers actually look for.

What the book covers:
  • Core structure of a strong business plan and why order matters
  • How to write a clear, concise executive summary
  • Translating your business model into realistic financial projections
  • Common red flags that lenders and investors notice immediately
  • Practical tips for tailoring your plan to banks, investors, or immigration
Who it’s for:
  • Entrepreneurs preparing their first serious business plan
  • Immigration applicants who must submit a business plan as part of their application
  • Consultants, accountants, and advisors who support clients with planning

Financial Due Diligence Essentials

Coming Soon

A practical guide to analyzing numbers before you invest, lend, or acquire.

Financial Due Diligence Essentials is designed to help readers go beyond surface-level financials and understand the story behind the numbers. It focuses on what lenders, investors, and acquirers actually check when they review financial statements, tax returns, and projections for a business.

What the book will cover:
  • How to read financial statements with a due diligence mindset
  • Quality of earnings, cash flow, and working capital analysis
  • Red flags in revenue, expenses, and owner compensation
  • Linking business risks to financial risks
  • Practical checklists for lenders, investors, and advisors
Who it’s for:
  • Entrepreneurs preparing for a bank, investor, or buyer review
  • Accountants, bookkeepers, and business advisors
  • New lenders or analysts learning to assess small and mid-sized businesses
