About Atul Jagga

Atul Jagga is the founder of The Biz Plans, a Toronto firm founded in 2016.
He holds the CPA (Ontario) and RCIC-IRB credentials.
His work spans business strategy, finance and Canadian immigration.

“I work at the intersection of business strategy, finance, and Canadian immigration.”

— Atul Jagga

Credentials and education

Atul’s credentials bring together accounting, immigration consulting, finance, strategy, marketing and operations. His academic qualifications include two MBAs: one in Finance & Strategy from the University of British Columbia, and one in Marketing & Operations from Panjab University. His professional credentials and completed levels are listed individually below.

  • CPA (Ontario)
  • RCIC-IRB — licensed immigration consultant, licence R711106
  • MBA, Finance & Strategy — University of British Columbia
  • MBA, Marketing & Operations — Panjab University
  • CFA Level I
  • CBV Level II

The Finance & Strategy MBA and the Marketing & Operations MBA represent distinct areas of his education. Alongside those degrees, his CPA (Ontario) credential sits within his finance background, while his RCIC-IRB credential relates to his work in Canadian immigration. CFA Level I and CBV Level II are identified here by their respective levels.

For visitors looking for Atul’s background in one place, this list brings his education and professional credentials together with the experience, authorship and engagement approach described below.

Experience across finance, strategy and immigration

Atul has more than 20 years of experience across corporate finance, fractional CFO advisory, business strategy and Canadian immigration. These are the areas at the centre of his professional background and of the intersection he describes in his own words.

His experience includes senior finance roles at Moneris, Payfirma and Idea Cellular. At Idea Cellular, he managed a revenue portfolio of approximately $150 million. Moneris, Payfirma and Idea Cellular are the organisations identified here in connection with his senior finance roles.

His project work involved RBC, BMO and CIBC. These organisations are mentioned solely in relation to that project involvement. The senior finance roles described above and this project work are separate parts of his experience.

Atul founded The Biz Plans in 2016 in Toronto. His background encompasses both corporate finance and fractional CFO advisory, alongside business strategy and Canadian immigration. The combination is reflected in his positioning: work at the intersection of business strategy, finance and Canadian immigration.
