Vancity Business Financing & Business Plans

Exploring business financing through Vancity? A well-developed business plan and financial model can help explain the financing request, market opportunity, operating strategy and projected financial performance.

Understanding Vancity Business Financing

Vancity is a credit union and The Biz Plans is an independent planning service. Business-financing options and supporting information may differ according to the business stage, financing purpose, amount, applicant circumstances, available financial information and Vancity's lending assessment.

Because products, membership considerations, rates and eligibility can change, confirm current details directly on Vancity's official Business Banking website before applying.

Preparing a Business Plan for Vancity Financing

Depending on the financing request, business and applicant circumstances, a lender may need to understand the business background, ownership and management experience; the amount requested, owner contribution and use of funds; products or services, target market, competition and operating strategy; and key risks.

Where available, historical results can provide context for forecast assumptions, profitability, cash flow and repayment capacity. The plan should make clear which statements are supported facts and which are management assumptions. For broader preparation guidance, see the Canadian Bank Loan Business Plan Guide and How Banks Evaluate Business Plans.

Connecting the Financing Request to the Business Plan

A coherent request follows a traceable sequence: financing amount → use of funds → business activity → financial impact → cash flow → repayment.

For example, a request might relate to equipment, leasehold improvements, a business acquisition, expansion, inventory, technology or working capital. These are planning examples only; they do not indicate that Vancity finances every category or that an applicant is eligible.

Businesses operating in the Lower Mainland can also review our Vancouver market and financial-planning approach, including how location, occupancy, hiring and seasonality can affect the assumptions presented to a decision-maker.

Financial Projections for Business Financing

Projections may model revenue assumptions, gross margins, staffing and payroll, operating expenses, capital expenditures, inventory and working capital, financing proceeds, interest, principal repayments and cash balances. The narrative business plan and financial model should reconcile so that timing, funding and operating assumptions tell the same story.

Common Business Financing Situations

Starting a Business

Business concept, start-up costs, market opportunity, owner investment and launch assumptions.

Buying a Business

Purchase structure, historical performance, transition strategy and post-acquisition projections.

Business Expansion

Expansion rationale, additional capacity, staffing, capital investment and projected sales.

Working Capital

Operating cycle, inventory or receivables where relevant, cash requirements and repayment capacity.

Each situation is illustrative and does not imply automatic eligibility for Vancity financing.

Information That May Support a Business Financing Application

Depending on the request, useful information may include a business plan, financial projections, historical financial statements, recent operating information, ownership information, financing details, and other relevant records. An acquisition may also involve a purchase agreement; an equipment or premises request may involve quotations, lease information and project costs. Confirm the actual documentation required with Vancity.

Frequently Asked Questions About Vancity Business Financing & Business Plans

Do I need a business plan for Vancity business financing?

Documentation depends on the financing product, business and applicant circumstances. A plan may help explain the request and repayment case, but confirm Vancity's current requirements directly.

What should a financing-focused business plan include?

It should connect the amount and use of funds to the business strategy, market evidence, operations, management, risks, forecast assumptions, cash flow and repayment capacity.

Do I need financial projections?

Projections may be requested or useful depending on the application. They should show how operating assumptions, financing, expenses and repayments affect profitability and cash.

Can you prepare projections for a start-up?

Yes. We can build projections from documented launch costs, pricing, sales, staffing, margin, expense and working-capital assumptions supplied and reviewed with the client.

Can you prepare a business plan for buying an existing business?

Yes. The scope can address the purchase structure, historical performance, transition plan, future operations, financing request and post-acquisition projections.

Can you work with my accountant?

Yes. With your authorization, we can coordinate relevant historical information and assumptions with your accountant while keeping project roles clear.

Does a professional business plan guarantee financing?

No. A professional plan can present the request clearly, but it cannot guarantee financing, approval, eligibility, terms or a particular amount. Vancity makes its own lending decisions.

Where can I find Vancity's current financing information?

Visit Vancity's official Business Banking website for current product, membership, rate, eligibility and application information.
