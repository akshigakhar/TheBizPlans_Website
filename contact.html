Frequently asked questions about Get in Touch With The Biz Plans

Practical answers to help you evaluate this topic and prepare your next step.

What happens after I submit the contact form?

The Biz Plans team will get back to you about the details you shared.

What should I bring to the business plan consultation?

Be ready to describe who will read the plan, what decision it needs to support, your deadline, how much funding you are seeking if applicable, and what records you already have.

How do I book a consultation from this page?

Select Book a Consult to choose a time through Calendly. You can also call the team directly or start a WhatsApp conversation using the contact options on this page.

What does the contact form ask me to provide?

Enter your name, email address, phone number and a message. If you are choosing a service, you can review our business plan writing services before sending your message.
