Desjardins Business Loan Business Plan

Supporting Entrepreneurs, Co-operatives, and SMEs Across Canada

Desjardins is the largest financial co-operative in North America and a leading provider of business financing in Québec and Ontario. With more than 7.5 million members and clients, Desjardins combines the stability of a major financial institution with a community-driven approach to banking. Its business loans and credit solutions are tailored to help entrepreneurs, professionals, and co-operatives finance start-ups, manage growth, and invest in modernization.

👉 Desjardins Business Financing

What Is a Desjardins Business Loan?

Desjardins offers flexible financing options for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), agricultural producers, and co-operatives. Its business loans are structured around your goals — whether you need funds to buy equipment, acquire property, expand operations, or strengthen working capital. Each application is evaluated on your project’s viability, repayment capacity, and contribution to your region’s economic development.

Types of Desjardins Business Financing

  1. Business Loan
    Provides long- or short-term financing to cover equipment purchases, expansion, or modernization projects. Features competitive fixed or variable rates and repayment schedules aligned with cash flow.
    👉 Business Loan Overview
  2. Line of Credit
    Offers continuous access to funds to manage day-to-day expenses, pay suppliers, or handle seasonal cash-flow variations. Interest is payable only on the funds used.
    👉 Business Line of Credit
  3. Equipment Financing and Leasing
    Enables entrepreneurs to purchase or lease new or used equipment through flexible and tax-efficient options. Ideal for manufacturers, agricultural producers, and service businesses investing in modernization.
  4. Commercial Real Estate Financing
    Long-term funding for purchasing, constructing, or renovating commercial and industrial properties. Repayment schedules are adapted to revenue streams and project timelines.
    👉 Commercial Real Estate Financing
  5. Agricultural and Agri-Food Loans
    Custom-designed credit solutions for farmers, agri-producers, and food processors through Desjardins Agricultural Financing. Includes financing for land, equipment, and operational needs.
    👉 Agricultural Financing
  6. Co-operative and Social Enterprise Financing
    As a co-operative institution, Desjardins actively supports financing for co-ops, social enterprises, and non-profits that contribute to local economies.
  7. Government and Partner Programs
    Desjardins collaborates with programs such as:
    • Canada Small Business Financing Program (CSBFP)
    • Investissement Québec and regional development funds
    • Futurpreneur Canada for young entrepreneurs
    👉 Government and Partner Programs – Desjardins

Who Can Apply

  • Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) across all sectors
  • Professionals and self-employed individuals
  • Co-operatives, non-profits, and social enterprises
  • Agricultural and agri-food producers
  • Entrepreneurs launching new businesses or acquiring existing ones

Eligibility is based on your project’s financial soundness, equity contribution, and business plan.
