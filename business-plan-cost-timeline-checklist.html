Business Plan Cost, Timeline and Preparation Checklist

A practical scoping guide for entrepreneurs comparing professional business-plan services in Canada.

What determines business plan cost?

There is no responsible one-price answer for every plan. Pricing depends on the intended reader, required research, business complexity, forecast depth, number of entities or locations, source-data quality and deadline. A lender plan for an operating company is different from a pre-revenue investor plan or an immigration submission.

A realistic project timeline

  1. Discovery and scope: define the audience, outcome, evidence and deadline.
  2. Information collection: gather ownership, operations, market and financial inputs.
  3. Research and modelling: test market assumptions and build the forecast.
  4. Draft and review: reconcile the narrative and numbers, then collect feedback.
  5. Final delivery: incorporate the agreed review round and prepare final files.

The schedule is confirmed after discovery; urgent delivery may reduce research or review time and should be discussed before work begins.

Business plan preparation checklist

Business inputs

  • Ownership and management biographies
  • Products, services and pricing
  • Location, suppliers and staffing
  • Customer and competitor evidence

Financial inputs

  • Funding request and capital contribution
  • Historical statements, if operating
  • Quotes for major purchases
  • Sales, margin and payroll assumptions

Questions to ask a business plan writer

  • Who writes and reviews the financial model?
  • What research and revisions are included?
  • How are assumptions documented?
  • Can the writer explain inconsistencies and risks rather than hiding them?
  • Does the scope match the actual lender, investor or reviewer?

Frequently asked questions about Business Plan Cost, Timeline and Preparation Checklist

Practical answers to help you evaluate this topic and prepare your next step.

What does Business Plan Cost, Timeline and Preparation Checklist include?

The scope is tailored to the decision the work must support and may include discovery, market evidence, strategy, operating assumptions, financial analysis and review. Confirm the specific deliverables in the project proposal.

Who is this service best suited for?

It is most useful when an owner or organization needs a structured, evidence-based document for planning or an external decision. The intended reader and stage of the business determine the appropriate depth.

What should a client prepare before starting?

Prepare the objective, deadline, ownership and management details, product or service information, available research and financial records. Identify estimates clearly so they can be tested rather than presented as facts.

How are financial assumptions developed?

Assumptions should be built from pricing, demand, capacity, staffing, cost quotes and cash timing. Material inputs are documented and reconciled across the narrative and financial schedules.

How long does the work take?

Timing varies with complexity, research needs, source-data readiness and review cycles. A realistic schedule can be confirmed only after the required deliverables and available information are assessed.

Does professional support guarantee a successful outcome?

No. The relevant lender, investor, government body or management team makes the decision. Professional work improves clarity and analytical consistency but does not guarantee approval, funding or performance.
