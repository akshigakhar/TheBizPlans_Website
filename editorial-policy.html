Editorial Policy and Review Standards

How we separate education from advice, verify time-sensitive information and keep commercial content accountable.

Purpose and editorial independence

Our educational content helps readers understand business planning, financial modelling and the documents decision-makers may request. It does not replace lender, legal, accounting or immigration advice. The Biz Plans is independent and does not claim affiliation with the banks, government programs or organizations discussed.

Research and source standards

  • Time-sensitive program facts should be checked against the responsible government, lender or program administrator.
  • Material claims should distinguish published criteria from our professional interpretation.
  • Examples and projections must be labelled as examples and must not be presented as guaranteed outcomes.
  • Commercial pages must explain both the service and its limitations.

Author and reviewer

Founder Atul Jagga brings CPA (Ontario), MBA and RCIC-IRB credentials together with corporate-finance and business-planning experience. Content involving financial models is reviewed for internal consistency; immigration and financing content directs readers to the responsible primary source for current criteria.

Update and correction process

Pages covering changing programs display a review notice where appropriate. We update substantive errors, broken primary-source links and outdated terminology when identified. Readers can report a concern through the contact page.

Commercial transparency

The website markets professional business-plan services. Calls to action are kept distinct from eligibility or approval statements. No plan, forecast or consulting engagement can guarantee financing, investment, nomination, a work permit or another third-party decision.

Frequently asked questions about Editorial Policy and Review Standards

Practical answers to help you evaluate this topic and prepare your next step.

What does Editorial Policy and Review Standards cover?

This page explains the applicable website or service policy in plain language. Read the complete policy because headings and FAQs are summaries and do not replace the governing terms on the page.

Who does this policy apply to?

It applies as described in the policy text, including any stated distinctions between website visitors, prospective clients and clients. Contact The Biz Plans if your circumstances are not addressed clearly.

Does this FAQ replace the full policy?

No. The full policy controls. These questions help readers locate common topics, but they do not add to, remove or amend the policy language.

How can I ask a question about this policy?

Use the contact details shown on this website and identify the policy and issue in question. Do not send unnecessary confidential or sensitive information in an initial message.

Can this policy change?

The policy may be updated as practices or requirements change. Review the version available on the website when you use the site or engage a service.

Links to privacy, terms and refund information appear in the website footer. Read all policies relevant to your interaction or engagement rather than relying on one page in isolation.
