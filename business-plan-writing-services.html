What does a business plan writing service include?

A complete engagement produces a written business plan and integrated financial projections built around the same assumptions. The document includes an executive summary, company overview, market and competitor research, and a plan for marketing, operations and implementation. The projections show how the business expects to perform over multiple years. The plan documents the assumptions behind those figures and explains how much funding the business needs and how it will use the money. Any funding schedules or supporting appendices are agreed as part of the scope.

How we develop and review your plan

We start by confirming who will read the plan, what decision it needs to support and what you’ll receive. What changes for each reader

  • Lenders need to see how much the business wants to borrow, what the money will pay for and what the owner will contribute. The cash-flow projections help the lender assess whether the business can repay the loan.
  • Investors need to understand the market opportunity, how the team plans to grow the business and what milestones the requested capital will fund. The forecasts show the assumptions behind that growth strategy.
  • Immigration officers assess the business proposal and its supporting evidence against the requirements of the specific pathway. For eligible immigration engagements, we tailor the plan to the business, program and application stage, checking time-sensitive criteria against official sources.

Custom business plan writing services

Business plan development starts with the evidence about your business and the decision the plan must support. Our professional business plan writing services connect original market and competitor research with operating strategy and financial modelling. Your quote defines the research and deliverables.

We examine your customers, competitors, pricing, capacity and costs. Those findings inform your marketing strategy and financial forecast. Quality business plans make that connection clear. We document sources and assumptions so you can explain how market evidence supports the operating plan and how the operating plan drives the projections.

The intended reader shapes the work. A lender-facing plan connects the funding request, use of funds and repayment assumptions. For an immigration business plan, we tailor the commercial narrative and financial schedules to the relevant program and agreed document requirements. For a U.S. venture, review our U.S. business plan services to identify the appropriate lender or immigration scope.

What's included in a custom engagement

  • Bank-financing plans — built for term loans, operating lines, and CSBFP-backed borrowing. The projections are structured the way underwriters read them, with debt-service coverage front and center. The written case also explains the financing request, use of funds, operating plan, and repayment source so the reader can trace the request through the numbers. Learn more about bank loan business plans.
  • Investor and funding plans — narrative plus financials for angel, seed, and partner-buy-in rounds. Clear use of funds, clear return logic, no hockey-stick fantasies. The plan connects milestones and the capital required to reach them, while keeping assumptions visible for diligence and discussion.
  • Acquisition and growth plans — for buyers acquiring a business and operators planning expansion. Purchase-price justification, integration costs, and combined projections. The work distinguishes historical performance from forward-looking assumptions and identifies the operating changes behind the forecast.
  • Integrated financial projections — three-year income statement, cash-flow statement, and balance sheet that tie together. Assumptions documented, so a lender can follow the math. Revenue drivers, staffing, operating expenses, capital requirements, financing, and cash timing are developed as one model rather than disconnected schedules.
  • Market research — competitor mapping, demand sizing from named sources, and positioning. Real research, not adjective paragraphs. Sources and dates make the evidence reviewable, while the analysis explains what that evidence means for customers, pricing, capacity, and the forecast.

Why this matters: most cheap “business plan writers” sell you a formatted document. What gets funded is the financial logic underneath it — and that's the part a CPA builds differently.

How a custom engagement runs, step by step

  1. Discovery call. We learn what the money is for — loan, raise, acquisition or immigration-related purpose — because the plan's structure depends on the audience. A bank plan and an investor plan are different documents. We also identify the decision-maker, requested amount where relevant, available records, and the questions the completed plan must answer. We confirm the scope, deliverables and review cycle before work begins.
  2. Research, strategy and financial modelling. We build your projections from your actual numbers — quotes, wage rates, rent, pipeline — plus market research from named sources. The research informs positioning, sales channels and operating choices as we develop the narrative and model together. Where an input is still an estimate, it is treated as an assumption to test and document rather than disguised as a fact.
  3. Draft and review. You review the draft, we revise. The financials have to reflect your reality, so your input here is what makes the plan defensible in a lender meeting. Review also checks that the operating plan, market evidence, funding request, and model tell the same story.
  4. Final delivery for the intended reader. A finished document with projections, funding request, and appendices — ready to submit, and ready for the questions that follow submission. The sequence is complete only when key assumptions can be located and explained by the founder who will present the plan.

The written plan explains your business model, strategy, marketing and operations. The financial model connects income, cash flow and the balance sheet. Funding schedules and appendices reflect the intended reader and agreed scope. We review and refine the work before delivery.

What's included in a professional business plan?

The full document: executive summary, market analysis, company description, organization, products and services, marketing and operations plans, three-year financial projections (income, cash flow, balance sheet), and the funding request. Every plan we deliver includes documented assumptions, so a lender can follow the math instead of taking it on faith. Frequently asked questions

What does a business plan writing service include?

The agreed scope can include market and competitor research, a written business plan, integrated income, cash flow and balance sheet projections, and documented assumptions. The plan and model are reviewed together before final delivery.

How long does it take to write a business plan?

Standard bank loan plans are usually completed in a few weeks. Immigration and investor plans that need deeper research or more complex financial modelling may take longer. We estimate the schedule after reviewing your project brief or speaking with you.

What do lenders need to see in a business plan?

Lenders typically want to see how much you are borrowing, how you will use the funds and how the business expects to repay the loan. Tie the request to cash flow projections, document the assumptions behind the forecast and confirm the lender’s checklist.

What do immigration officers need to see in a business plan?

Requirements vary by program. The plan should explain how the proposed business will operate and support that narrative with market evidence, investment and staffing plans where relevant, and consistent financial projections. Use the applicable program requirements to guide the supporting documents.
