Project Description Format

What to Include

Use this outline to describe your project clearly. It helps us scope work accurately for banks, investors, and immigration submissions.

1) Overview

A short summary of the business, offering, and context (new venture, expansion, acquisition, or immigration).

  • Business name, location, and stage
  • Products/services and target market
  • Funding/immigration objective (e.g., CSBFP, BDC, C11, ICT)
2) Objectives

What success looks like in 6–24 months.

  • Revenue, unit sales, locations, or approvals targeted
  • Key milestones (launch, hiring, equipment, partnerships)
3) Deliverables

What you need from us.

  • Business plan (bank, investor, or immigration)
  • Financial model (3 statements, DCF, sensitivities)
  • Pitch deck, market research, appendices
4) Timeline

Important dates and dependencies.

  • Desired first draft and final delivery dates
  • Third-party lead times (e.g., franchise FDD, leases, quotes)
5) Budget

High-level project budget (capex/opex) and funding mix.

  • Total project cost & sources (debt/equity/grants)
  • Working capital needs
6) Risks & Assumptions

Top uncertainties and constraints we should model.

  • Seasonality, supply, pricing, hiring, or regulatory factors
  • Critical assumptions for scenarios/sensitivities
7) Contacts & Files

Who will review and approve; any reference documents.

  • Lead contact, stakeholders, accountant/lawyer (if any)
  • Upload quotes, leases, FDDs, historicals, or market data
