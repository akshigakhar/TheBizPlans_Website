New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program (NBPNP)

Business Immigration Stream — CPA-built financials, NB market validation, and job-creation timelines.

The NBPNP — Business Immigration Stream is built for experienced entrepreneurs who will establish, purchase, or partner in a New Brunswick business. Candidates typically start on a work permit to launch and operate their venture and, after meeting their Business Performance Agreement, may be nominated for permanent residence.

Official Government page:
👉 NBPNP — Business Immigration Stream

Program Overview

Administered by New Brunswick’s Department of Post-Secondary Education, Training and Labour, the stream targets entrepreneurs who bring capital, innovation, and local jobs to the province.

Program details:
👉 Immigrate to New Brunswick — Business Immigration

Eligibility & Investment Requirements

  • Net worth: minimum CAD 500,000 verified by a province-approved third-party accounting firm
  • Investment: minimum CAD 150,000 in an eligible NB business
  • Ownership: at least 33.3% (or CAD 1,000,000 investment for a smaller share)
  • Experience: ≥ 3 years business ownership or ≥ 5 years senior management in the last 10 years
  • Age: 21–59; Education: high-school diploma or higher
  • Language: CLB/NCLC 5 (English or French)
  • Residency & management: live in NB and actively manage the business
  • Exploratory visit: required before submitting an EOI

Eligibility details:
👉 NBPNP Business Immigration — Eligibility

What Provincial Officers Look For

  1. Economic contribution: sector diversification, innovation, and capital at risk
  2. Job creation: at least one full-time position for a Canadian or PR
  3. Business viability: defendable market research and realistic forecasts
  4. Active management: day-to-day involvement in operations
  5. Community impact: local hiring, supply-chain links, regional growth
  6. Financial capacity: sufficient funds to launch and sustain
  7. Settlement intent: genuine plan to reside in New Brunswick

Applications are ranked on a points system covering experience, concept quality, investment, and adaptability.

Why a Strong Business Plan Is Essential

  • Clear business concept, ownership structure, and NB location strategy
  • Market research grounded in New Brunswick’s economy and demographics
  • Operations & staffing plan with defined roles and wage budgets
  • 3–5 year financials: revenue, costs, and cash flow
  • Job-creation timeline aligned to local labour conditions
  • Implementation milestones from lease/licensing to launch and growth

How to Apply — NBPNP Business Immigration

  1. Complete an exploratory visit to New Brunswick.
  2. Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) with your business concept.
  3. Upon selection, file a full application and third-party net-worth verification.
  4. Attend an interview if invited.
  5. Sign the Business Performance Agreement and obtain a work-permit support letter.
  6. Apply to IRCC for a work permit and establish the business in NB.
  7. After meeting all terms (investment, jobs, operations), apply for nomination and then permanent residence.
