Helping Entrepreneurs Access Financing and Grow Their Businesses with Confidence

TD Canada Trust is one of Canada’s largest banks, offering a full suite of business financing solutions designed to help entrepreneurs start, expand, and manage their operations.

From working-capital loans to equipment financing and real-estate purchases, TD provides flexible credit options and personalized financial advice to support business growth at every stage.

What Is a TD Small Business Loan?

TD’s business-financing programs provide entrepreneurs with the capital needed to cover day-to-day operations, purchase equipment, or invest in growth. TD offers both term loans and lines of credit, allowing businesses to choose between fixed repayment schedules or revolving credit for ongoing flexibility. These products can be customized based on your cash-flow cycle, credit profile, and business objectives.

Types of TD Business Financing

  1. TD Business Term Loan
    Provides a one-time lump sum for major purchases such as equipment, vehicles, or facility renovations, with fixed or variable rates and predictable monthly payments.
    👉 TD Business Term Loan
  2. TD Business Line of Credit
    Offers ongoing access to capital to manage working capital, seasonal expenses, or short-term cash-flow needs.
    👉 TD Business Line of Credit
  3. TD Agriculture Lending Solutions
    Financing for agriculture directly through TD.
    👉 TD Agriculture lending solutions
  4. TD Equipment Financing
    Custom solutions for purchasing new or used equipment, tailored to your repayment ability and asset life.
  5. TD Commercial Mortgage
    Supports the purchase, expansion, or refinancing of commercial properties, with flexible amortization and repayment terms.
    👉 TD Commercial Mortgage – Small Business

Who Can Apply

  • Canadian-owned businesses with active operations and revenue generation
  • Start-ups with a solid business plan and financial projections
  • Established companies expanding operations, purchasing assets, or improving cash flow
  • Entrepreneurs with strong personal and business credit history

👉 TD Business Account Eligibility and Requirements

Loan Amounts and Terms

While each TD loan is tailored to the borrower’s needs, typical features include:

  • Loan amounts ranging from $10,000 to $1,000,000+ depending on credit strength
  • Repayment terms up to 10 years for term loans and flexible revolving access for lines of credit
  • Fixed or variable interest rates linked to the TD Prime Rate
  • Flexible payment structures aligned with business cash-flow cycles
