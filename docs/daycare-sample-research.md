## Official-source register

The Ontario pages blocked full-page retrieval (403) through the web tool. Current indexed excerpts on the named official Ontario pages supplied the regulatory checks; none of the numeric rules relies on an unofficial summary. The CRA memorandum was retrievable in full. Search-result research and citations were checked on the date above; dynamic requirements must be rechecked for the actual application.

- Ontario, **Child care rules in Ontario**: https://www.ontario.ca/page/child-care-rules-ontario . Current indexed official table confirms infant younger than 18 months: 3 employees to 10 children, maximum 10; toddler 18 months to under 30 months: 1 to 5, maximum 15; preschool 30 months to under 6 years: 1 to 8, maximum 24. The age-range boundaries are also supported by the regulation and licensing glossary below.
- Ontario, **O. Reg. 137/15, Schedule 1**: https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/150137 . Cross-check for age ranges, ratios and group sizes. Historical version URLs appearing in search were not used as the published canonical source. The current official Ontario 2025 annual report Table 20 corroborates these categories: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontarios-early-years-and-child-care-annual-report-2025 .
- Ontario Ministry of Education, **Child Care Centre Licensing Manual, Part 3 — Ratios and Group Size**: https://www.ontario.ca/document/child-care-centre-licensing-manual/part-3-ratios-and-group-size . Explains ratio/group framework and exceptions. The sample deliberately does not budget on reduced-ratio exceptions. The glossary confirms the ordinary toddler/preschool boundaries: https://www.ontario.ca/document/child-care-centre-licensing-manual/glossary-terms .
- Ontario, **Child Care and Early Years Act, 2014**: https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/14c11 . Licensing framework. The rules page explains licensed centres versus licensed home child care agencies. The sample does not publish home-care capacity numbers.
- Ontario Ministry of Education, **Apply for or renew a child care licence**: https://www.ontario.ca/page/apply-or-renew-child-care-licence . Required approvals, service-system-manager contact and process. Search indexes an approximate duration, but the requested FAQ is qualitative and no duration is promised or published.
- Ontario Ministry of Education, **Licensing Manual, Part 4 — Building, Equipment and Playground**: https://www.ontario.ca/document/child-care-centre-licensing-manual/part-4-building-equipment-and-playground . Municipal checks, premises and playground framework. No numeric indoor/outdoor space minimum is published in the sample because the premises-specific application must be reviewed.
- Ontario Ministry of Education, **Licensing Manual, Part 8 — Staff Qualifications**: https://www.ontario.ca/document/child-care-centre-licensing-manual/part-8-staff-qualifications . RECE status, College register and supervisor/qualified employee requirements. No numeric qualification fraction or minimum wage is published. The sample requires a verified roster; the staffing envelope is explicitly illustrative and is not proof of compliant coverage.
- Ontario, **Canada-Ontario early learning and child care agreement**: https://www.ontario.ca/page/canada-ontario-early-years-and-child-care-agreement . System overview and reduced parent fees. **Licensing Manual, Part 2 — CWELCC**: https://www.ontario.ca/document/child-care-centre-licensing-manual/part-2-canada-wide-early-learning-and-child-care-cwelcc . Funding, fee treatment and enrolment obligations.
- Ontario Ministry of Education, **Ontario Child Care and Early Years Funding Guidelines, Chapter 2, Division 2: CWELCC Cost-Based Funding Guideline, April 2026**: https://www.ontario.ca/files/2026-04/edu-chapter-2-division-2-cwelcc-cost-based-funding-guideline-en-2026-04-29.pdf . Cost-based funding framework. Official April 2026 financial-reporting guidance also identifies eligible costs and base-fee revenue: https://www.ontario.ca/files/2026-04/edu-standardized-financial-report-and-cost-reviews-requirements-en-2026-04-29.pdf . The page describes the framework qualitatively and does not quote a statutory fee cap or guaranteed payment formula. Its $400/month parent receipt and annual operator funding are independent hypothetical assumptions, not regulated fees or a reimbursement of the private-fee gap.
- Canada Revenue Agency, **GST/HST Memorandum 21-1, Child Care Services**: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/21-1/child-care-services.html . Supports care/supervision of children 14 years or under for periods normally less than 24 hours per day as generally exempt. These are the only other regulatory numbers published. Exempt is not zero-rated; the page cautions against assuming purchase-tax credits and recommends separate classification of other supplies.
- Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada, **CSBFP Guidelines**: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-small-business-financing-program/en/canada-small-business-financing-program-guidelines . Equipment and leasehold-improvement categories. The sample quotes no maximum entitlement and does not assert that the full request or its cash reserve qualifies under one category. Lender assessment remains necessary.
