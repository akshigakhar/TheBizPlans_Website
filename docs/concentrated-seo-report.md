# Concentrated SEO ranking-growth report

**Implementation date:** 1 September 2026
**Strategy:** concentration + authority + existing ranking momentum

## Executive summary

This pass prioritizes commercial ownership and the supplied Semrush position ranges rather than publishing a large article set. The directional positions are not treated as live Google rankings; Search Console should replace them in the tracker when current query/URL data is available. Search volume was not present in the repository data supplied for this pass and is therefore recorded as `Not supplied`, rather than estimated.

The eight-factor opportunity model considered position, volume availability, commercial intent, service relevance, current page quality, internal-link opportunity, extractable-answer opportunity and conversion potential. The resulting order is: (1) commercial terms in positions 11–30, (2) commercial/top-10 defence, (3) lender terms in positions 11–30, and (4) informational feeders. No mathematical score is published because several inputs are qualitative or unavailable; the `Commercial Priority` classification in the tracker records the actionable outcome without creating false precision.

## Changes implemented

### Tier A money pages

**Competitor-validated P0:** repository-supplied competitor research reports approximately 390 monthly searches and $9.38 CPC for `business plan services`, with Adept and Mikel observed around positions 1 and 2. These figures are directional snapshots, not live-rank guarantees. They make `/services.html` the clearest commercial gap.

- `/services.html` remains the unambiguous owner of **business plan writing services**. Its title, H1 and answer-first opening now lead with the `business plan services` / `business plan writing services` family, while an immediate contact action and visible Service schema reinforce the offer. Existing sections already cover audience, scope, deliverables, purpose, engagement process and contact, so unrelated educational copy was not added.
- `/` remains the owner of **business plan writer** and related consultant language. Its existing optimized substance was preserved.
- Vancouver and Toronto remain isolated geographic owners. No national or neighbouring page was retargeted and no unsupported office claim was introduced.

### Position 11–30 / AEO lender work

Five established lender/program pages received one concise, answer-first section apiece. Each uses a question heading, a direct 40–80-word answer, a verification or lender-discretion caveat and a restrained bridge to `/services.html`:

- RBC: business-plan/document expectations without claiming a universal RBC rule.
- BDC: common preparation requirements without claiming approval criteria are identical for every product.
- TD: application documentation and internal consistency.
- Scotiabank: information that may support a financing request.
- CSBFP: eligibility ownership and the participating lender's independent credit decision.

These additions preserve the established pages instead of replacing their useful lender analysis. They also supply contextual commercial authority from five high-relevance pages to services.

### Linkable assets

`/business-plan-template.html` now offers four genuinely downloadable PDF worksheets: construction, trucking, daycare and beauty salon. It explains how to adapt the files, what a plan normally includes, and why a generic template may not be enough for a consequential financing decision. It links naturally to the generator and professional service.

`/business-plan-generator.html` defines the TheBizPlanAI workflow, limitations and best use cases. Because a working application was not present in the repository, the page clearly says access is being prepared and offers a launch-list contact route; it does not pretend that a non-existent tool is live. It links to templates for immediate DIY use and services for higher-stakes work.

Contextual links were added from five strong educational/decision pages: business-plan essentials, chapter 1, industry plans, plan comparisons and the ideal-plan article. The existing sample page also routes users to both assets. Anchor text varies naturally by context.

## Internal-link audit outcome

The earlier repository-wide audit found that all established indexable pages are discovered through the shared static navigation/footer. This pass therefore avoided mechanically placing six targets on every page. It selected contexts where the next step is useful:

- Educational structure and ideal-plan content → template and generator.
- Industry hub → industry PDFs, generator and services.
- Comparison/sample content → the appropriate DIY asset or professional alternative.
- Lender/program guides → services.
- Template → generator → services.

The previous focused authority sets remain in place: 10 sources to services before this pass, 6 to the homepage, 4 to Vancouver and 5 to Toronto. The five lender bridges plus proof-, expertise- and modelling-page additions provide especially strong contextual support to services. The bank-loan service now also completes the financing → lender preparation → plan → projections → professional assistance path. Existing homepage/city links were not inflated merely to meet a quota.

## 90-day priority plan

The percentages below are resource-allocation guidance, not ranking factors or outcome promises.

### Days 1–30: validate, recrawl and prepare outreach

- **40% quality backlink/outreach:** build a vetted list of Canadian entrepreneurship organizations, colleges, accountants, advisers, chambers and industry associations. Match each prospect to one useful asset; do not send generic bulk requests.
- **30% commercial and positions 11–30:** submit updated money/lender URLs for recrawl; replace directional positions and missing volumes with current Search Console/Semrush exports; inspect query-to-URL overlap weekly.
- **15% assets:** QA PDF downloads, gather user feedback and define the real generator release requirements.
- **15% AEO/PAA:** validate rendered headings and track the five new answer blocks without expanding them into FAQ spam.

### Days 31–60: earn authority and refine from evidence

- Pitch the template library to the most relevant resource curators, tracking acceptance, referring domain, destination and anchor type.
- Review impressions, CTR and average position for each tracker row. Improve title/opening only where query and URL evidence shows a mismatch; preserve 80–95% of useful Top-10 page substance.
- Launch TheBizPlanAI only after its promised workflow is functional. Then update availability schema and replace the wait-list CTA with the real product action.
- Test 2–5 high-value PAA questions only on pages that already satisfy the intent. Cite current official lender/program sources during editorial review.

### Days 61–90: consolidate winners

- Direct further outreach toward assets that earn qualified visits or editorial links; discontinue low-fit campaigns.
- Compare 28-day periods with deployment annotations. Assess clicks, CTR, position, enquiries and assisted conversions together—not position alone.
- Investigate cannibalization only when Search Console shows multiple URLs repeatedly receiving the same query. Consolidate or re-anchor based on evidence, not keyword similarity alone.
- Refresh time-sensitive lender statements against official sources and record review dates. Do not infer lender requirements.

## Measurement protocol

Use `docs/seo-ranking-tracker.csv` as the baseline framework. Update current position and volume from the same source/date at regular intervals; keep directional values labelled until replaced. Target stages (`TOP 3`, `TOP 10`, `TOP 20`) are monitoring bands, not guarantees. Record links by distinct contextual source page, and record external links by qualifying referring domain rather than raw link count.
