Annotated E2 business plan sample

Executive summary and operations

Fictional sample. A proposed neighborhood coffee shop would serve commuters and nearby residents from a leased storefront. The fictional owner would manage the launch, with opening contingent on completing the fit-out and obtaining applicable permits.

What officers look for. Show how the enterprise will sell goods or services for profit. Distinguish completed steps from planned ones and tie the opening schedule to premises, permits, equipment and supplier records. Use local demand and competitor evidence to support projected customer traffic.

Investment amount and at-risk funds

Fictional sample. The estimated startup cost is $100,000. The fictional investment schedule shows $30,000 paid for equipment and $50,000 committed under a binding fit-out contract. The remaining $20,000 is a future operating reserve held in cash and is excluded from the paid and committed total.

What officers look for. They assess substantiality relative to the cost of buying or establishing the enterprise and consider whether funds are genuinely committed and exposed to commercial loss. Reconcile the startup budget, lawful source and path of funds, contracts, invoices and payment records. Have counsel review which expenditures and commitments qualify.

Ownership and the investor’s role

Fictional sample. The fictional investor would own and manage the shop, approve spending, negotiate supplier terms, set prices and supervise hiring. A shift lead would handle day-to-day shift coverage as demand grows.

What officers look for. Explain how the investor will develop and direct the business. Tie decision-making authority to ownership and governance records, and show who handles day-to-day work on the organization chart.

Staffing and marginality

Fictional sample. The fictional shop would budget for two part-time baristas at launch and add shift-lead coverage in Year 3 if traffic and cash flow support the additional payroll.

What officers look for. Show the enterprise’s present or future capacity to provide more than a minimal living for the investor and family, or to make a significant economic contribution. Explain when jobs are needed, what each role costs and how operating cash will support payroll. E-2 guidance sets no universal hiring count.

Five-year financial projections

Fictional sample. The illustrative sales forecast starts with 80 daily transactions in Year 1 and assumes annual increases of 10 transactions per day through Year 5. At 300 operating days per year and an average sale of $8, the illustrative annual revenue is shown below.

What officers look for. A credible forecast links revenue to local demand and operating capacity. Build five years of income statements, cash-flow forecasts and balance sheets. Carry the investment into opening cash, assets and any unpaid obligations. Budget rent, supplies and payroll, then test whether lower traffic delays hiring or exhausts cash.

E2 visa business plan template checklist

Start with the free business plan template pack for the general outline. Add these E-2 sections and tailor the supporting records to your enterprise.

  • Executive summary and company description. Identify what you sell, where you operate, who owns the enterprise and what needs to happen before launch.
  • Market and operations. Document local customers and competitors, explain how you will win sales and map the milestones for opening or expanding.
  • Investment and evidence. Reconcile the cost of the enterprise with lawful funding sources, transfers, payments, binding commitments and future spending.
  • Management and staffing. Document control, the investor’s duties and projected hires, with start dates, hours and wages tied to operating needs.
  • Financials and marginality. Add the five-year statements and assumptions, test a downside scenario and explain what the forecast shows about income capacity or economic contribution.
  • Supporting documents. Index ownership records, market sources, contracts, invoices, payment evidence and schedules so readers can trace each major claim.

Frequently asked questions

What should an E2 visa business plan template include?

Include an executive summary, ownership and management, market research, an operating plan, an investment schedule, a hiring plan, five-year financial projections and an index of supporting documents.

Where can I download a free E2 visa business plan template?

Use the free business plan template pack linked above for the general outline and add the E-2 investment, ownership, staffing and evidence sections listed on this page.

How should I use this E2 business plan sample?

Use the fictional excerpts to see how plan statements tie to evidence. Replace all example assumptions, figures and roles with your own documented business facts and have your U.S. immigration attorney review the completed plan.

Does the investment in this E2 business plan sample establish a minimum?

There is no fixed dollar minimum. Officers assess the investment relative to the cost of purchasing or establishing the enterprise and consider what has been committed and placed at commercial risk.

What five-year projections should I add to an E2 visa business plan template?

Include an income statement, cash-flow forecast and balance sheet for each of the five years. Document sales, costs and payroll assumptions, and show how slower sales would affect cash and hiring.
