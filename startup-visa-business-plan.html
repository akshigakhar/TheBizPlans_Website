Canada Startup Visa Business Plan

Evidence-led business planning with CPA and RCIC-IRB context.

A Canada startup visa business plan explains how a proposed venture can serve customers, operate in Canada and fund its development. What designated organizations expect in the plan

IRCC’s published designated-organization list includes venture capital funds, angel investor groups and business incubators. See IRCC’s designated organizations (checked October 26, 2026). Each organization uses its own evaluation process; the following are business-planning recommendations, not a universal IRCC checklist.

Explain the customer problem and why the product offers a useful solution. Identify target buyers, competing alternatives, pricing and a practical route to market. Customer interviews, pilot results and clearly labelled expressions of interest carry more meaning than unsupported market-size claims. Distinguish completed validation from future research.

Connect founder experience to responsibilities in product development, selling, operations and financial control. Document Canadian activities, staffing, dependencies and milestones. Show what funding makes possible, what remains uncertain and what changes if a pilot or financing round is delayed. A persuasive pitch and the detailed plan should use the same commercial assumptions.

Financial projections for SUV business plans

Build revenue from identifiable drivers: customer numbers, contract values, conversion assumptions, renewals and delivery capacity. For an early-stage venture, explain development costs and the evidence behind the expected commercialization date. Avoid treating a large addressable market as proof of near-term sales.

An integrated model should connect the income statement, cash-flow forecast and balance sheet. Include payroll, contractors, marketing, premises, technology, equipment and working capital. Document founder contributions and distinguish committed capital from prospective funding. Model a slower launch and lower sales so the cash requirement remains visible.

Keep business funding separate from household settlement resources. IRCC’s published eligibility page requires unborrowed settlement funds appropriate to family size and states CLB 5 in listening, reading, writing and speaking in English or French. These published criteria do not mean intake is open. Verify IRCC’s language and settlement-fund requirements (checked October 26, 2026). We do not reproduce the settlement-dollar table because IRCC updates it; use its live table for the relevant family size.

How the plan supports the letter-of-support application

In the published SUV process, the organization assesses the venture, issues a letter of support if it decides to support it, and sends a commitment certificate to IRCC. Read IRCC’s description of organizational support (checked October 26, 2026). This explanation is context for existing files; new intake is paused.

The plan supports commercial due diligence by making assumptions traceable. Keep the pitch, ownership narrative, founder roles, implementation schedule and funding model consistent. Maintain a dated evidence register so a reviewer can distinguish the original proposal from subsequent changes. An existing applicant responding to questions should reconcile updates with the previously submitted material and obtain advice about the specific file.

Frequently asked questions

Is Canada’s Start-Up Visa Program accepting new applications?

No. IRCC reports the program was paused on June 30, 2026. Previously accepted applications continue to be processed. Status checked October 26, 2026; consult IRCC for subsequent changes.

What do designated organizations expect in a business plan?

Expectations differ by organization. A useful plan documents the customer problem, product, market evidence, founder capabilities, Canadian operations, milestones and financial assumptions. Check the organization’s own review process; there is no universal endorsement template.

What financial projections should an SUV plan include?

The agreed scope may include revenue assumptions, operating costs, payroll, capital spending, income statements, cash flow, balance sheets and downside scenarios. Keep personal settlement funds separate from venture funding.

How does a plan support a letter-of-support review?

It organizes the commercial evidence an organization can assess and helps reconcile the pitch, ownership, milestones and funding. It does not replace the organization’s independent decision or reopen paused intake.

How does Atul’s CPA and RCIC-IRB background inform the work?

Atul connects financial modelling and business evidence with immigration context. The engagement defines the planning deliverables and whether separate immigration representation is included. Decisions remain with the responsible organizations and government officers.
