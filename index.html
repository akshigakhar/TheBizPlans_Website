Purpose-built, not generic

Business Planning for Your Next Decision

A strong plan must work for its intended reader. Our business plan consultants connect your strategy, market evidence, operating plan and financial assumptions in one clear document. Engagements are customized to the business stage, decision-maker and financing or growth objective—not filled into a standard template.

Depending on scope, an engagement may include discovery, market and competitor research, operating strategy, a written business plan and integrated financial projections. For financing work, the plan can also set out the funding request, sources and uses of funds, cash-flow requirements and repayment assumptions. The written plan and forecast are developed together, then reviewed for consistent assumptions through the agreed revision process before the final documents are delivered.

Our work includes startups, established businesses seeking financing or expansion, business acquisitions, investor and funding situations, standalone financial modelling and immigration-related business plans where applicable. Hiring a business plan writer: common questions

What does a professional business plan writer do?

A writer develops the business narrative, supporting research and financial projections around the intended reader. The work connects market evidence, operations and financial assumptions, with deliverables and review defined by the engagement scope.

What does it cost to hire a professional writer?

Cost depends on the research, modelling, business complexity and review required. Ask for a scoped quote and compare the included deliverables rather than page counts. Fees are confirmed for the particular engagement.

How long does business plan preparation take?

Preparation depends on the scope, available records, research needs and review cycles. Discuss your deadline and missing inputs during the assessment so an appropriate schedule can be agreed. There is no single turnaround that applies to every plan.

What should I look for when hiring a business plan writer?

Check relevant credentials, who leads the engagement, how research sources and assumptions are documented, and whether the narrative and model are reviewed together. Confirm the deliverables and revision process before work begins.

Do you write plans for immigration and lender use?

The Biz Plans prepares lender-facing plans and immigration-related business plans where applicable. Scope depends on the lender or relevant program and the business circumstances. Preparing a plan does not guarantee financing or immigration approval; the relevant decision-maker assesses the application.
