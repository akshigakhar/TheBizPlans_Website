Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions about The Biz Plans

Answers for evaluating a professional business plan company and preparing to begin.

Who does The Biz Plans work with?

We support entrepreneurs and businesses across Canada that need a customized plan for bank financing, investors, an acquisition or an eligible immigration pathway. The appropriate scope depends on the business stage and intended reader.

Who prepares and reviews the business plan?

Atul Jagga leads the work with CPA-level financial analysis and business planning experience. The narrative, market evidence, assumptions and financial schedules are reviewed for consistency according to the agreed scope.

Are your business plans based on a standard template?

No. A consistent quality-control structure may be used, but the research, strategy, assumptions and financial model reflect the individual venture, objective and audience.

What information is needed to begin?

Share the intended reader, purpose, deadline, business model, funding target and available financial or operating records. A consultation or concise project brief helps identify the right scope and any evidence gaps.

How are project timing and fees determined?

Timing and fees depend on scope, complexity, research depth, forecast requirements, data readiness and review needs. The deliverables, quote and schedule are confirmed before work begins.

Can a professional business plan guarantee funding or approval?

No. Banks, investors, government bodies and other reviewers make independent decisions. Professional support can improve clarity and analytical consistency, but it cannot guarantee an outcome.
