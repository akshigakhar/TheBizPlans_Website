Professional Business Plan Writing Services

A strong plan must work for its intended reader. Our business plan consultants connect your strategy, market evidence, operating plan and financial assumptions in one clear document. Engagements are customized to the business stage, decision-maker and financing or growth objective—not filled into a standard template.

Depending on scope, professional business plan writing can include discovery, market and competitor research, strategic analysis, financial modelling and a consistent final narrative. Explore our business plan writing services to choose the right starting point.

Experience behind every engagement

Professional Business Plan Writers and Consultants

Your business plan writer should understand both the narrative and the numbers. Founder Atul Jagga leads engagements with more than two decades of relevant finance, consulting and business-planning experience. He is a CPA (Ontario), RCIC-IRB and holds an MBA in Finance and Strategy from the University of British Columbia.

Our business plan experts review the relationship between strategy, research, assumptions and financial outputs. That disciplined process helps clients present a coherent, decision-ready case without promises of funding or approval.
